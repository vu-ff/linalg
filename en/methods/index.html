How to solve the standard problems of Lectures 0–6

Methods (PDF) Problems

Most computational problems of the first lectures are solved by one algorithm: write a matrix, reduce it to RREF, and read the answer off the pivots. The problems differ only in which matrix you write and what you read off. The elimination steps are written out once, in Lecture 0; here they are marked by a single → RREF \xrightarrow{\ \text{RREF}\ } RREF ​ arrow. Each method ends with the sheet problems it solves.

Choosing the matrix

Vectors go into the columns; equations go into the rows. A bar appears only when the question names a target. Vectors of any other space are first replaced by their coordinate vectors in one fixed basis; for polynomials and matrices, the standard one (Example 2.15).

1 Linear systems

Lecture 0

Problem. Solve Ax=bAx=bAx=b: decide whether it has solutions, and write them all. For b=0b=0b=0 this asks for a basis of the null space ker⁡A\ker AkerA.

Method

  1. Reduce [A∣b][A\mid b][A∣b] to RREF. A row [ 0⋯0∣c ][\,0\cdots0\mid c\,][0⋯0∣c] with c≠0c\neq0c=0: no solution.

  2. Set every free variable to 000 and read off a particular solution xpx_pxp​.

  3. Take b=0b=0b=0. For each free variable, set it to 111 and the other free variables to 000, and read off one vector. These vectors are a basis of ker⁡A\ker AkerA.

  4. The solution set is xpx_pxp​ plus any linear combination of the basis of ker⁡A\ker AkerA.

3 Independence; reducing a list to a basis

Lecture 2

Problem. Is v1,…,vmv_1,\dots,v_mv1​,…,vm​ independent? If not, which vectors are redundant, and what are a basis and the dimension of the span?

Method

  1. Write v1,…,vmv_1,\dots,v_mv1​,…,vm​ into the columns, in the order of the list; reduce.

  2. The list is independent iff every column has a pivot.

  3. A column without a pivot is redundant: its entries are its coefficients in the pivot columns to its left.

  4. The pivot columns of the original list are a basis of the span; the dimension is the number of pivots.

Why step 4 works. Deleting the redundant vectors does not change the span (Lemma 2.10). The same row operations take the remaining columns to distinct standard vectors eie_iei​, so they are independent.

Find a basis and the dimension of their span.
